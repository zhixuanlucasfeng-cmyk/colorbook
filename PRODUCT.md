# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web (iPad PWA at repo root) + WeChat mini program (`mp/`)

## Users
The owner (and later friends / 拼豆 hobbyists) relaxing with color-by-number on iPad or phone. Short sessions, picking up half-finished pieces.

## Product Purpose
Turn any photo into a numbered pixel grid and color it in. Exists because the popular apps are ad-heavy, lock colors, and look blurry. Success = calm, ad-free coloring of your own photos at high detail.

## Positioning
Your own photos, not a stock gallery; up to 800-cell grids and 64 colors; every color editable; no ads ever.

## Constraints
- No ads, no dark patterns.
- Works offline; all processing on device.
- Chinese UI copy.
